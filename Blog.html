INSIGHTS, TIPS, AND STORIES TO GUIDE YOUR CAREER JOURNEY

How to Prepare for Job Shadowing

By Admin | August 22, 2025

  • Research the company and the role you will be shadowing.
  • Dress appropriately and bring a notebook to jot down key insights.
  • Be curious! Ask questions politely and show enthusiasm.
  • Reflect afterwards on what you learned and how it fits your career goals.

Career Tips for Students

By Admin | August 22, 2025

  • Focus on building your skills, not just getting grades.
  • Volunteer or join clubs to gain experience.
  • Network with teachers, professionals, and peers.
  • Keep your CV updated.
  • Explore different career paths! You don’t have to have it all figured out yet.

Why Job Shadowing Matters

By Guest Writer | August 22, 2025

Many students think job shadowing is just about watching someone work, but it is much more than that. Job shadowing helps you see the day-to-day reality of a profession, the challenges people face, and the skills you need. It allows you to test whether a career is right for you before investing years of study. For employers, it is also a chance to meet motivated students who could become future employees.
