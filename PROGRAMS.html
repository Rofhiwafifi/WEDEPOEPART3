PROGRAMS

1. Industry Shadowing Program

  • Healthcare Shadowing: Spend the day with doctors, nurses, or lab technicians.
  • Technology & IT Shadowing: Observe software developers, network engineers, or data analysts.
  • Business & Finance Shadowing: Learn from accountants, managers, or entrepreneurs.
  • Creative & Media Shadowing: Work alongside designers, writers, or media professionals.

2. Mentorship Program

  • Pair students with professionals in their field of interest.
  • Weekly virtual meetings.
  • Career guidance, CV review, and interview practice.

3. Career Bootcamp

  • Weekend camps focusing on specific industries.
  • Hands-on activities: mock projects, role-playing workplace scenarios.
  • Networking events with local professionals.
